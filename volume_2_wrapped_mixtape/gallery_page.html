Volume 3: 2025 MIX(ed media)TAPE FOR ENEMIES OF THE STATE

2k25

I recently got to spend time with my friends Angel, Emily, Lynn, and Todd...but was severely depressed. When I returned home, I felt tremendous regret for having been unable to fully enjoy my time with them. In particular, I'd hoped to spend the time with Emily showing each other silly internet memes, as we often did when I lived in Florida. This series expresses the imagined meme-sharing I hoped for, with glitches added to mimic the glitching of my actual phone. Each image is framed as a symbol of artistic elevation; I intend this elevation to reference the heightened joy of sharing memes with friends, rather than strictly as an attempt to situate these images within any objective hierarchy of art's fine-ness.

This collection of images comes from my excessive thinking about the intersection of "fine art", zine culture, and memes-as-online-graffiti. Memes are a form of art we see constantly in online spaces, yet aren't really conceived of as art in many contexts. In part, I think this is because we don't think of "website" as a valid form of fine art in the same way we do "painting" or "film."

All images were found around the internet and edited by yours truly. I used a combination of making backgrounds transparent, altering the opacity of images or sections of images, and layering of glitch images for effect.
